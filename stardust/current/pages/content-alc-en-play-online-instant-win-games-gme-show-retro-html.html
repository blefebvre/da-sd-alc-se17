Game Show: Retro

$0.20 - $20

Bonus Offer

Game Summary

Cost: $0.20 - $20.00 Top Prize Base Game: $40.00 - $4,000
Progressive Jackpot Seed: Minor: $100. Major: $1,000 Epic: $10,000
Total Theoretical payout: 89% Overall odds: 1 in 3.85

Points: 2 points/$1 spent

  1. How to Play
  2. Prizes & Odds

Each Game Show: Retro tickets feature fifteen (15) symbols on a five-by-three (5x3) game board.

These are the ways to win:

·       CLUSTER WIN - Reveal three (3) or more identical symbols that share a side to automatically remove that cluster of symbols from the board and win according to the PRIZE TABLE. For each symbol removed a new symbol appears from above.

·       EVENT SYMBOL – Reveal the EVENT SYMBOL anywhere on the board to activate one (1) of four (4) possible features:

o   EXPANDING WILD – Substitutes for any symbol except for the EVENT, SURPRISE WIN or BONUS WHEEL symbols. WILD symbol can expand to the size of two-by-one (2x1) or three-by-one (3x1).

o   BONUS WHEEL – Reveal the BONUS WHEEL symbol under the EVENT SYMBOL to activate BONUS WHEEL and win the PRIZE shown on the board. The BONUS WHEEL expands and covers the symbols around it. Spin the wheel to collect CASH PRIZE, JACKPOT PRIZE or LEVEL UP. LEVEL UP will upgrade all CASH and JACKPOT prizes on the wheel and grant one additional spin. There can be up to two (2) LEVEL UP options and up to three (3) wheel spins in a given round. The prize range for CASH PRIZE in BONUS WHEEL is between x10-x200.

o   RESPIN – Reveal a RESPIN symbol under the EVENT SYMBOL to reset the board with new symbols for another chance to win. The prize range for RESPIN is between x3-x75.

o   SURPRISE WIN – Reveal a SURPRISE WIN symbol under the EVENT SYMBOL to win a surprise amount instantly. The prize range for SURPRISE WIN is between x2-x50.8.

See in game for prize structure.
