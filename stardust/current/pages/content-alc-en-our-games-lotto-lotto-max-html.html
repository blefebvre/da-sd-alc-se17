Lotto Max - How To Play

How to Play LOTTO MAX

LOTTO MAX is  Canada’s biggest jackpot game . The jackpot starts at $10 million, and if no one wins, it grows by $5 million increments each draw, up to $90 MILLION.  

There are two draws each week, held on Tuesday and Friday nights at 11:30 pm Atlantic Time.

Each $6 play includes four selections of seven numbers from 1 to 52. You can select your own numbers for the first selection, with the remaining three generated automatically, or select Insta Pik to have all four selections randomly generated. 

When the LOTTO MAX jackpot reaches $50 million, additional $1 million prizes, called MAXMILLIONS are added. The number of MAXMILLIONS prizes increases as the jackpot grows, giving you more chances to win.

NEW $100,000 prizes every draw! As the LOTTO MAX jackpot grows, so does the number of prizes — up to 90 prizes to be won. The bigger the jackpot, the more prizes and chances to win! 

How can I get a ticket?

Choose your numbers
• Insta Pik – all four selections of seven numbers are randomly selected for you. 
• Choose your own numbers – select your own numbers for the first selection; the remaining three selections are generated automatically
o Selection Slip – when playing at a retail location, fill out a LOTTO MAX selection slip to choose your numbers.

Where can I play?
Play Online
• Create an account on alc.ca or download the ALC Mobile App. 
• Buy tickets for 1 to 10 advance draws, or purchase a subscription and we’ll buy your tickets automatically for every draw.

Play at Retail
• Visit any local retail location to purchase LOTTO MAX tickets.
• Buy tickets for 1 to 10 advance draws, or use a self-serve terminal where available.

Buy your tickets on alc.ca, the ALC mobile app or at your local retailer to enter the draw. 

Looking to play more Lotto games? Check out our combos.

Did I Win?

Each selection of 7 numbers on your LOTTO MAX ticket gives you a  chance to win.
• Match all 7 numbers in one of your selections to win the jackpot.
• You can also win other prizes by matching 3, 4, 5, or 6 numbers, and with or without the bonus number.

$100,000 Prizes
Additional selections are also drawn for $100,000 prizes.
Match all 7 numbers in one complete $100,000 selection to win $100,000.

MAXMILLIONS Prizes
When available, additional MAXMILLIONS selections are drawn.
Match all 7 numbers in one complete MAXMILLIONS selection to win a $1 million MAXMILLIONS prize.

How Do I Know if I Won?
• Scan your ticket(s) with the ALC Mobile app to see if you’ve won. 
• If you purchased your ticket on alc.ca or the ALC Mobile app, we’ll email you if you win. You can also sign in and check the “My tickets” section of your account.
• Winning numbers are available on alc. ca, the ALC Mobile app and at retail locations after the draw.
• Visit any local retail location to validate your ticket.

If you win a Free Play ticket on alc.ca it will automatically be redeemed as a ticket placed in the next LOTTO MAX draw. You can also redeem your Free Play ticket any retail location.

Add-On games for LOTTO MAX tickets

When you buy your LOTTO MAX tickets we also offer you more ways to win.

You can purchase one of our add-on games.

TAG - is a $1 add-on game added to your ticket. It is a six-digit number between 100,000 and 699,999.

If your TAG number matches the six-digit winning number in the exact order drawn, you win the $100,000 TOP PRIZE! 
There are also other prizes available to be won.

Twist - is a $1 add-on that gives you two selections of 7 random generated numbers that are added to your ticket.

If your TWIST numbers match the LOTTO MAX winning numbers, you win the top prize of $ 100,000.
