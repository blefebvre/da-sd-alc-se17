Executive Profiles

DALLAS MCCREADY

                                                                         President and CEO

About DALLAS MCCREADY

Dallas McCready was appointed President and CEO of Atlantic Lottery in January 2025. Prior to accepting the role of President and CEO, Dallas served as the organization’s Chief Strategy Officer.
 
Dallas holds a diploma in Business Administration from New Brunswick Community College and brings a unique combination of public and private sector experience to his role. Before his time at Atlantic Lottery, Dallas worked with companies like Spielo Gaming, Brunswick News and most recently, Inversa Systems as Vice President of Business Development. His public sector experience as Deputy Minister, Strategic Initiatives in the New Brunswick Executive Council Office positioned him to advance Atlantic Lottery’s already strong relationship both with its provincial shareholders and other stakeholders, first in his CSO role and now as President and CEO.

Dallas was born and raised in New Brunswick and has spent the past 30 years living in Greater Moncton. Passionate about volunteerism and giving back to local communities, Dallas currently sits on the board of directors for the YMCA of Greater Moncton and Friends of the Moncton Hospital Foundation. He is also a member of the New Brunswick Business Council.

In addition to volunteering, Dallas enjoys playing sports, travelling and spending time with his friends and family in his spare time.

MICHAEL MACKINNON

Chief Player Experience Officer

About MICHAEL MACKINNON

Michael MacKinnon has led a diverse number of teams across Atlantic Lottery since joining the corporation in 2009. With experience in corporate planning, IT business operations, hospitality, Lottery, and Gaming, Michael brings a well-rounded corporate expertise to his role as Chief Player Experience Officer. Michael and his team are responsible for enhancing the player experience across all channels and brings a deep passion for Healthy Play to the position.

Michael was born and raised in Prince Edward Island and is a graduate of Holland College. He, his wife Michelle and son Alex have spent many happy years between New Brunswick and Prince Edward Island, both of which feel like home to their family.

An avid community volunteer, Michael has spent much of his free time over the years as a Rotary member, Junior Achievement PEI board member, Charlottetown Chamber of Commerce advocacy committee member, participant in the Georgetown Conference, as well as being involved in all aspects of Minor Hockey. Currently Michael volunteers as an Executive Board Member to his local golf club.

BRIAN LORDON

Chief Products & Technology Officer

About BRIAN LORDON

Brian Lordon joined Atlantic Lottery in 2019 as Vice President of Digital and was appointed to Chief Information Officer in 2021. Brian has since expanded his portfolio and now serves as the organization’s Chief Products & Technology Officer.

As Atlantic Lottery’s most senior leader in information technology, Brian is responsible for a talented team of people who implement world-class gaming and entertainment experiences for Atlantic Canadians. Brian’s objective is to make sure that Atlantic Lottery continues to leverage technology to stay a step ahead of the competition.

Brian has been using technology to help businesses compete for over 20 years.  He has held leadership roles at both J.D. Irving, Limited and Bell Aliant.  He has experience in business development, software development, finance, operations and research and development.  Brian is passionate about leadership, development of high-performance teams and partnering for success.

Born in Chatham, New Brunswick, Brian holds an MBA from the Sobey School of Business at Saint Mary’s University, a graduate diploma in applied I.T. and bachelor’s degrees in business and science (B.Sc. & BBA) from St. Francis Xavier University.

In his spare time Brian can be found with his wife and three kids in a rink, on a golf course, at a dance recital or at a baseball diamond.

 LEEANNE MULLINS

Chief People & Purpose Officer

About LeeAnne Mullins

LeeAnne joined Atlantic Lottery as Chief People & Purpose Officer in 2026, bringing a depth of well-rounded experience across every facet of human resources and people functions to the role.

LeeAnne and her team are responsible for organizational identity and workforce strategy, providing oversight for enterprise change management and ensuring people and purpose are embedded in Atlantic Lottery’s business strategy to drive meaningful and lasting impact.

Prior to joining Atlantic Lottery, LeeAnne served as a partner with EY in the Workforce Advisory practice, following several years with Deloitte leading large-scale culture, leadership and organizational transformation initiatives for clients across private, public and not-for-profit sectors. Earlier in her career, she held senior leadership roles at Comcast, leading enterprise-wide culture, talent, technology and change programs.

Born and raised in Cape Breton, LeeAnne now calls Moncton home. Outside of work, she and her husband enjoy travelling, discovering great food, and spending time with friends, often around a campfire. She is passionate about life in Atlantic Canada and is proud to apply her expertise to work in an organization that makes a positive impact across the region.

 MICHELLE JEWER

Chief Financial Officer

About Michelle Jewer

Michelle joined Atlantic Lottery in 2025 following more than 25 years with the Government of Newfoundland & Labrador. She held numerous financial leadership positions across various departments and served as Deputy Minister of Finance for four years. A graduate of Memorial University of Newfoundland, she holds a Bachelor of Commerce degree and is a Chartered Professional Accountant.

Michelle brings a depth of expertise and regional experience with her to this position and will play a key role in delivering on our commitment to making a game-changing impact for the people and places of Atlantic Canada.

MEREDITH KIDNEY

Vice President, Brand and Communications

About MEREDITH KIDNEY

Meredith Kidney was appointed Head of Brand and Communications at Atlantic Lottery in 2021. With over 10 years at the organization in numerous marketing and e-commerce roles, most recently leading the Lottery team as Head of Lottery, Meredith brings a breadth of knowledge and experience to the role.

Responsible for marketing, communications and corporate brand and reputation, Meredith and her team work to bring a unified voice to market across all disciplines.

Meredith holds a Bachelor of Business Administration from UNB Saint John, where she also started her career working for NBTel.

Now settled in Salisbury, N.B. with her husband and two children, Meredith is involved with local sport and school-related organizations that provide children in the community a sense of well-being and belonging. Meredith and her family love to spend their free time playing sports or doing anything that requires being outdoors.

Shannon French-Demille

Vice President, Products & Growth

About SHANNON FRENCH-DEMILLE

Shannon French-Demille has been with Atlantic Lottery since 2008, holding progressively senior roles across sales, marketing and product teams since that time. In 2025, she was appointed to her current role of Vice President, Products & Growth.

In this role, Shannon is responsible for leading AL’s product and distribution strategy, driving market share expansion and sustainable growth in a competitive and rapidly evolving industry.

Shannon holds a BBA in Marketing from Acadia University and an MBA in International Business from Dalhousie University.

Based in Riverview, N.B., Shannon enjoys spending her personal time with her husband and two children, is actively involved in sports and can often be found in a local rink or on a baseball field.

MAUREEN WOJICK

Vice President, Player Experience

About MAUREEN WOJICK

Maureen Wojick joined Atlantic Lottery in 1998 and has since worked in nearly every retail product and marketing area in the organization.  In her current role as Vice President Customer Engagement, Maureen brings several decades of leadership experience, product knowledge and marketing expertise.

Maureen holds a Bachelor of Public Relations degree from Mount Saint Vincent University and believes building strong teams and partnerships are key to delivering innovative solutions and great results.

She and her talented team help to guide the organization’s customer engagement approach with a strong focus on quality customer experiences and industry leading healthy play practices.  

Born and raised in beautiful Cape Breton, Maureen, her husband and their three sons have now called Moncton home for more than 25 years.  Maureen and her family cherish the Atlantic Canadian way of life and she takes great pride in being able to use her professional expertise the help drive business results that give back and have a meaningful impact in the Atlantic region.

JENNIFER RICHARDSON

Vice President, Risk & Assurance

About JENNIFER RICHARDSON

Jennifer joined Atlantic Lottery in 2016, bringing with her several years of experience in auditing and risk management. Since that time, she has held progressively senior roles on the Risk & Assurance team prior to her appointment of Vice President, Risk and Assurance in 2024. In this role, Jennifer oversees a team of risk and assurance professionals whose ongoing commitment to transparency and integrity supports the organization’s goals of providing regulated and reliable products to Atlantic Canadian adults. Jennifer also provides risk and assurance guidance to the Executive Team and the Audit Committee of Atlantic Lottery.  She reports directly to the Chair of the Audit Committee, ensuring the highest level of independence as required for her role.

Jennifer holds a Bachelor of Arts degree from Saint Thomas University and the Certified Internal Auditor (CIA) designation.

Outside of Atlantic Lottery, Jennifer and her husband enjoy spending their time outdoors, whether it’s on hiking trails, ski hills or early morning beach walks. 

 

ROB AUCOIN

Vice President, Technology

About ROB AUCOIN

Rob Aucoin joined Atlantic Lottery in 2009 and has since held several leadership roles within the Technology division, encompassing portfolio delivery, enterprise architecture, data services, and quality assurance. In 2025, he was appointed Vice President of Technology.

In this role, Rob leads the high-performing teams responsible for enabling Atlantic Lottery’s growth strategy through the delivery of player-centric technology solutions. He is accountable for the stability, resilience, and performance of all gaming and business technology platforms, ensuring alignment with organizational objectives and standards.

Rob earned degrees in biopsychology and kinesiology from the University of New Brunswick before transitioning into the technology sector, later completing a post-graduate diploma at the Information Technology Institute (ITI). He recently earned the CIO.D designation from the CIO Association of Canada, reinforcing his commitment to continuous professional development. Rob is an active member of Atlantic Canada’s TechImpact Community and collaborates with fellow technology leaders through the New Brunswick Digital Leadership Forum. Throughout his career, he has played a key role in delivering complex transformational initiatives and remains passionate about building strong partnerships and empowering teams to excel.

While he is now based in Moncton, N.B., Rob will always proudly consider Cape Breton, N.S. to be his true home. Outside of work, he maintains an active lifestyle, enjoys travel, and values time spent with family and friends.
