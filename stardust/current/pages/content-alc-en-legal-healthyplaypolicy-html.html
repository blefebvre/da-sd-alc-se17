Alc.ca Healthy Play and Self-Exclusion Policy 

 

1.0 INTRODUCTION

The Atlantic Lottery Corporation (“ALC”) Healthy Play and Break in Play Policy (“Healthy Play Policy”) describes the Healthy Play (“HP”) program, interactions with Players, and the tools and resources that are available both to Players, and, as well, to members of the public in Atlantic Canada should they decide to register as a Player on ALC.ca. 

 

Note: A “Player” is an individual who has successfully registered on ALC.ca following verification that the person is at least 19 years of age and a resident of Atlantic Canada. A Player is not permitted to have more than one ALC.ca account.
 

This Healthy Play and Break in Play Policy forms part of the Terms of Service for ALC.ca. Additional information regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information may be found here. Capitalized terms that are not otherwise defined in this Healthy Play Policy have the meanings ascribed to them in the Terms of Service. ALC may periodically update this Healthy Play Policy and will provide notification of any updates on ALC.ca, however, Players are responsible for remaining familiar with the terms of this Healthy Play Policy over time.

2.0 ALC’s HEALTHY PLAY PROGRAM

 

2.1 Tools, Resources and Information ALC’s Healthy Play Policy and HP program is based on, and has been developed using, research evidence, expert guidance and jurisdictional comparisons. It is supported by employee and retailer training, Player educational programs and initiatives and marketing programs. The HP program is designed to encourage healthy play with the goal of providing Players with tools, resources, and information they can use to help themselves make responsible and informed gambling decisions and to prevent and/or mitigate gambling-related risks and harm. 

Healthy Play is typically characterized by playing for fun and entertainment with an understanding of how the games work, being able to decide how much time to spend, and playing within a Player’s means. ALC’s HP program enables Players to choose a play experience that works best for them. ALC has a focus on measuring positive gambling-related beliefs and behaviours as an overall indicator of healthy play in Atlantic Canada.

ALC’s HP program offers a range of Player education resources, healthy play tools and resources, including the Break in Play program (detailed below).

2.2 Player Responsibility Based on responsible gambling research and a policy in favour of empowering individuals, ALC’s policy is to minimize involuntary restrictions on Players. Instead, ALC’s HP program focuses on informing and supporting Players and on providing Players with tools to manage their own behaviour. ALC’s policy is that the choice of whether or not to gamble ultimately rests with each individual Player, and therefore the choice of whether or not to use any of the ALC HP tools or resources also ultimately rests with each individual Player. Players at all times are solely responsible for their gambling activities, on ALC.ca or otherwise. This Healthy Play Policy is not an acknowledgement of any obligation to Players on behalf of ALC to control, limit, prevent, or otherwise modify Player’s gambling behaviour, on ALC.ca or otherwise. 
 

2.3 No Representations ALC makes no representation or warranty, whether expressed or implied (whether by applicable law or otherwise), in respect of ALC’s HP program, or any component thereof, or any HP or responsible gambling tools on ALC.ca, or the capabilities thereof, or as to the accuracy or completeness of any information contained or used in such programs or tools. ALC will not be responsible for any losses as a result of or in connection with any Player’s use, misuse, or attempted use of any component of ALC’s HP program or any of the responsible gambling tools on ALC.ca.

 

3.0 HEALTHY PLAY TOOLS AND RESOURCES

 

3.1 Information The HP resources and tools described in this Section 3 are available to Players on ALC.ca to support Players’ efforts to engage in healthy play behaviours, as well as learn about and understand their own gambling activity, patterns, and behaviours.

Players can find further information about the HP tools available on ALC.ca here and in the Play Responsibly and My Limits sections of My Account available after a Player signs into their ALC.ca account.

3.2 Player Education and Awareness Both Players and the public can access healthy play information and resources, including (i) information on game features such as prize structures, odds, payout percentages/return to Player and how the games work,

(ii) “Know the Facts” - facts or “myth-busters” that dispel common gambling misconceptions, (iii) gambling risks and warning signs that could indicate a gambling problem, and (iv) problem gambling help resources. This information can be accessed in the PlayWise Know the Facts section on ALC.ca.

3.3 Maximum Weekly Deposit Limit Prior to depositing funds to a Player Account, Players are required to establish a Maximum Weekly Deposit Limit, which will run each week from 12:00 a.m. Sunday to 11:59:59 p.m. the following Saturday (Atlantic Time). If a Player registers for an ALC.ca Account and establishes a Maximum Weekly Deposit Limit after 12:00 a.m. Sunday, for the week in question the limit will apply to 11:59:59 p.m. the following Saturday. Subject to any limits established by ALC, Players may increase their weekly Deposit Limit at any time; however, this increase will only take effect following a 24-hour period. Players may decrease their weekly Deposit Limit at any time with immediate effect.

Note: Promo Cash does not apply towards the Maximum Weekly Deposit Limit.

Prior to setting a Maximum Weekly Deposit Limit, a Player has the option to view an instructional video explaining how the tool works and the intent of the tool to support Players in staying within their budget.

Annually, ALC will provide Players an on- screen reminder upon log in that will remind Players of the amount of their Maximum Weekly Deposit Limit and require that each Player confirm or update their limit, prior to the Player being able to resume playing Games.  

3.4 Daily Wager Limits Players can set a maximum Daily Wager Limit, in each Player’s discretion. Players will not be able to wager any amount on ALC.ca beyond the Daily Wager Limit each Player has established during each one-day period from 12:00 a.m. to 11:59:59 p.m. (Atlantic Time). Subject to any limits established by ALC, Players may increase or remove their Daily Wager Limit at any time; however, this increase or removal will only take effect following a 24-hour period. Players may decrease their Daily Wager Limit at any time with immediate effect. Promo Cash wagered is not applied towards a Daily Wager Limit.

 

3.5 Session Time Limits At any time, Players may set a limit on the total time they spend per session playing on ALC.ca. Players will be notified via a pop-up reminder five (5) minutes before the Player’s allotted session time limit. A subsequent pop-up message will be displayed to Players one (1) minute before reaching the Player’s limit.

If the time limit Players set is reached for a session, Players will not be able to purchase, wager or play any Games on ALC.ca for the next 24-hour period (Players will be logged out of ALC.ca; Players can log back in but will not be able to purchase, wager or play any Games for the next 24-hour period).

The time for Session Time Limits begins following Players logging into their ALC.ca Account. Idle time between purchasing, wagering or playing any Game counts toward the accumulating time (including any time playing bonus or free game). The only point that the timer stops is when Players log out or are logged out of their ALC.ca Account.

Players may increase or remove their Session Time Limit at any time; however, this increase or removal will only take effect following a 24-hour period. Players may decrease their Session Time Limit at any time with immediate effect.

Session Time enables Players to keep track of how much time they have spent logged onto ALC.ca.

Players will be notified via a pop-up reminder every 60 minutes that Players are logged into ALC.ca. Players must acknowledge this pop-up reminder each time it appears before Players can continue to use or interact with ALC.ca.

3.6 Mandatory Break in Play All Players are automatically logged off ALC.ca immediately after being logged in for a continuous five (5) hour period. Players are notified five (5) minutes and one (1) minute prior to this automatic log off and are responsible for completing any Game play prior to this automatic log off.
 

3.7 Self-Assessment At any time Players can take or re-take a self-assessment test to obtain information about their gambling behaviour. The assessment is for informational purposes only (it is not a clinical assessment of any kind) and Player’s answers and test results are not accessed or stored by ALC. The results Players obtain from using the Self-Assessment tool may be different from results from the PlayWise Rating tool, as these assessment tools operate differently. Self-Assessment is self-reported based whereas PlayWise generates rating based on a Player’s actual play and related activities.
 

3.8 PlayWise Rating Players understand and agree that ALC, through an independent third party, does engage in play tracking and analysis of any Player who signs up for an Account on ALC.ca and, depending on Player activity on ALC.ca, a confidential risk rating may also be generated by an algorithm used by the independent third party. Players can access their risk rating in the “Play Responsibly” section of “My Account”. The PlayWise Rating information is for informational purposes only and cannot always correctly identify problem gambling. The PlayWise Rating information is not a clinical assessment of any kind however it may be relied on for the purposes listed in the paragraph immediately below. 

 

Depending on a Player’s PlayWise risk rating, Players understand and agree that ALC may, but is not required to: (i) provide information to assist in promoting HP behaviour and/or available support resources, (ii) adjust marketing and/or promotional activities, (iii) tailor communications to and/or with Players for HP purposes (iv) adjust product offerings, and/or (v) make other adjustments from time to time as appropriate. Players understand and agree that ALC may use information from PlayWise for primary or third-party research purposes, in accordance with ALC’s Online Privacy Policy. 
 

3.9 Outreach Players understand and agree that Players who explicitly express gambling-related harm or distress, such as directly stating concerns or difficulties in their communications with ALC through telephone, email or social media, may be identified by ALC for proactive outreach. In such cases, the outreach may be conducted either by ALC or an independent third party and may include providing the Player with information on responsible gambling tools, support services and relevant resources. This outreach is intended to promote Player well-being and facilitate access to appropriate assistance services where needed.

The Player may be contacted directly by telephone or email using the telephone number and/or email address indicated in the Player’s ALC.ca Account. Players are solely responsible for ensuring that this contact information is accurate, up to date and appropriate for receiving communications of this nature. 

3.10 Account History A history of transactions is available to Players in My Account. The history includes deposits, wins, withdrawals and game play history.
 

3.11 Breaks in Play A Break in Play is intended to provide a Player with a short-term or long-term break in Game play, should the Player decide to take a Break. See Section 4.0 for information on accessing Breaks in Play.

4.0 BREAK IN PLAY AND REINSTATEMENT PROGRAM

4.1 Short-Term Break in Play A Player has the option to take a “Short-Term Break in Play” for a range of 1 day to 90 days. A “Short-Term Break in Play" allows Players to take a break from playing any Games on ALC.ca by deciding to exclude themselves from being permitted and able to log-in to their ALC.ca Account and play Games for a period of between 1 to 90 days.

4.2 Long-Term Break in Play A “Long-Term Break in Play" allows Players to take a break from playing any Games on ALC.ca by deciding to exclude themselves from being permitted and able to log-in to their ALC.ca Account and play Games for a period of (i) six (6) months; (ii) twelve (12) months; (iii) twenty-four (24) months; or (iv) thirty-six (36) months.

4.3 Restrictions

 If a Player goes on either a Short-Term or Long-Term Break in Play, the Player during their confirmed Break in Play period will not be able to:

1. sign-in to the Player’s Account;

2. play any Games (Subscriptions purchased before the Break will continue, however 4-week subscriptions will not automatically renew); 

3. (i) earn Promo Cash, AL Rewards points or entries for any promotional contests from ALC.ca or (ii) use or redeem Promo Cash or AL Rewards points on ALC.ca or otherwise or submit/assign entries into promotional contests on ALC.ca (NOTE: any Promo Cash, AL Rewards points and earned promotional contest entries that are not expired and otherwise valid can be used, redeemed and submitted, as applicable, upon the expiration of the Break in Play and applicable reinstatement referred to in paragraph 4.5, unless the particular program/promotion specifies otherwise, but only for programs/promotions in effect after the applicable reinstatement referred to in paragraph 4.5. In addition, if an entry submitted/assigned into a promotional contest prior to registering for any Break in Play is determined to be a winning entry, any associated contest prize will be awarded in accordance with that contest’s term and conditions and ALC’s policies and procedures);

4. end the Break in Play. 

4.4 Other Terms

1. Marketing Materials ALC will suspend sending marketing emails to Players who opted into receiving them prior to taking any Break in Play during their confirmed Break in Play period. Upon the restrictions for a Short-Term Break being removed in accordance with paragraph 4.5.1, marketing emails from ALC to the Player will automatically resume. Upon the restrictions for a Long-Term Break being removed in accordance with paragraph 4.5.2, Players are required to manually opt in to receiving marketing emails in their Account Profile prior to ALC resuming sending these emails to Players. NOTE: Due to system processes, Players should expect to continue to receive marketing emails and communications for up to approximately 24 hours from the time they initiate any Break in Play.

2. One Account Only Players are never permitted to open more than one (1) ALC.ca Account, during any Break in Play or otherwise.

3. Return of Funds Upon request in the case of Short-Term Breaks in Play and automatically in the case of Long-Term Breaks in Play, ALC will return to the Player who confirmed a Break in Play the balance of funds that exists at the time of confirming a Break in Play, in accordance with ALC policies and this Agreement.

ALC may require the Player who confirmed a Break in Play to provide additional information and/or documentation that ALC determines necessary before disbursing any funds. Additional information may be found here.

4. No Refund Completed ticket purchases for Draw-Based Games and Subscriptions will not be refunded for any Break in Play. In the event that a ticket for a Draw-Based Game purchased prior to registering for any Break in Play (including purchased by way of Subscription) is determined to be a winning ticket, any Prizes associated with that ticket will be paid in accordance with ALC prize claim policies and procedures. 

5. Take a Break Link Players may choose to take a Break in Play by accessing the Take a Break link wherever it appears on ALC.ca. Information regarding Break in Play (and Breaks) can be accessed on the Break in Play page in the PlayWise section on ALC.ca. After signing in to their ALC.ca account, a Player can access Break in Play (and Breaks) by choosing the PlayWise Tools section followed by the Take a Break button, and also by choosing the Take a Break on all game wager screens.

4.5 Return to Play/Reinstatement

1. Short-Term Upon the expiration of a Short-Term Break in Play, Players must review the Healthy Play content on the “Return To Play” screen and provide the required acknowledgement indicating that they wish to return to play prior to the restrictions set out in paragraph 4.3 above being removed.

2. Long-Term Upon the expiration of a Long-Term Break in Play, Players are required to participate in an online Return to Play Program in its entirety (which may be a video tutorial) and provide the required acknowledgement indicating that they wish to return to play prior to the restrictions in paragraph 4.3 above being removed.

5.0 RESPONSIBLE PRODUCT DESIGN

ALC uses a Social Responsibility Assessment (SRA) process to support alignment with ALC’s core values of offering products with integrity and responsibility. A key element of this process includes such things as ensuring healthy play tools and resources, as referred to in this Policy, are available to Players and ensuring that requirements such as session time reminders, mandatory breaks, and providing information to ensure transparency as how the product plays, prize payouts, avoiding gambling misconceptions, etc, are met.
