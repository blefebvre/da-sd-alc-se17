When you browse one of our sites or use one of the products and services offered by ALC, this Privacy Notice applies to you, which means that you accept its terms and conditions.

This Privacy Notice explains how we collect, use, or disclose your personal information. It also describes the choices you can make regarding your personal information and how to update your contact information. 

Our commitment to privacy is based upon the 10 principles outlined in Canada’s Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act (PIPEDA). 

1. What personal information do we collect? 

Personal information is information about you. It can be any information that you provided to us, such as your name, or information that can be linked to you, such as your IP address. The type of personal information we collect depends on how you interact with ALC. Review each section below to discover more about the type of personal information we collect.

Account Registration on ALC.ca

When you create an account with us, we require you to provide the following information: 

  • Your name

  • Your date of birth

  • Your telephone number and email address

  • Your street address (street number, street name, city, and postal code)

  • Your security questions and answers

  • Your (unique to you) password

  • Your driver’s license, passport, or other government-issued photo ID.

As part of the process of creating an account and using our services, we create a user account number that is uniquely associated with you. As you interact with our website, we also log what type of purchases, promotions, loyalty rewards, and events you have participated in. We also verify your identity through a credit check (Equifax or Transunion) or another method.

 

Online Casino and Sports 

If you choose to play our online games, we must verify your identity based on rules established by the Financial Transactions and Reports Analysis Center of Canada (FINTRAC). This means we will need to verify your identity through a credit check (Equifax or TransUnion) or another method to verify your name, date of birth, and street address. In some cases, we may also ask you to submit your driver’s license, passport, or other government-issued photo ID, and a photo (selfie) to verify your identity. We may also need to know your occupation. For FINTRAC requirements related to identity verification, please visit the FINTRAC website here. 

 

Financial Information

We will need your financial information, such as your bank account information, to award you certain prize(s). If you choose to fund your wallet on ALC.ca, we will record your chosen method of payment. If you choose to withdraw your money from your ALC.ca account, we will need your bank account information and may need to verify your identity.

 

Communication with ALC and Others 

When you communicate with ALC online, by email, or by telephone, we keep records of your communications. While communicating with ALC or others, this communication may be monitored and tracked for quality assurance, training, assistance, and security purposes. 

You may also choose to play certain games with us, which requires you to create a unique username to participate and communicate with ALC and/or other players. The information you provide in chat is also viewable to other players.

 

Other information that can be linked to you

When you visit our website (and have an account with us), we collect additional information that can be associated with you. This can include:

  • Your IP address and/or device ID.

  • The operating system and browser you used to access our website.

  • Your device-specific information, including the hardware model, operating system information, and mobile network information.

  • The internet service provider you used to visit our website.

  • Your geographic location.

  • Your account history, including online and retail purchases recorded on your ALC.ca account, play activity, loyalty rewards, and account updates relating to changes to your personal information and preferences.

  • The dates and times you accessed our website and the pages you visited.

 

Social Media

You may choose to communicate with ALC through social media channels. This may include posts, tags, or private messages to ALC. In the process of communicating with you, we may have access to your contact information. This includes:

  • Your name or username

  • Your social media profile

If you communicate with us using direct messaging, the conversation may be monitored for quality assurance, training, assistance, and security. 

 

Surveys or Contests

You may choose to participate in a contest or survey. If you choose to participate, we may ask you for your contact information. This includes:

  • Your name

  • Your email address

  • Your phone number

  • Your age range

  • Your photo

We may also need to verify your identity by collecting your driver’s license, passport, or other government-issued ID. If you participate in a contest or survey, you must accept both the terms and conditions of the contest or survey and the Privacy Notice. 

 

Winner Process

If you win certain games and contests, we may need you to provide the following information:

  • Your name

  • Your date of birth

  • Your telephone number and email address

  • Your street address (street number, street name, city, and postal code)

  • Your photo

We must also verify your identity by collecting your driver’s license, passport, or other government-issued photo ID. We may also need additional information related to the ticket, including but not limited to, the purchase, acquisition, and validation of the ticket, reasonably required to process prize claims.
