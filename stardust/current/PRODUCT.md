## Product Purpose

Atlantic Lottery presents legal, regulated lottery and online gaming to adults in Atlantic Canada. It lets visitors discover games and promotions, review winning numbers, learn how tickets and prizes work, access healthy-play tools, create or recover an account, and understand how lottery profits return to the region.

## Users

- Adults in Atlantic Canada choosing or playing lottery, casino, instant-win, sports, and subscription products.
- Ticket holders checking results or learning how to validate a ticket and claim a prize.
- Registered or prospective online players managing account access and rewards.
- People seeking healthy-play facts, self-assessment tools, and gambling support.
- Media, community partners, and residents looking for corporate, governance, and regional-impact information.

## Brand Personality

- **Regional and proud:** copy repeatedly ties the product and its profits to Atlantic Canada.
- **Optimistic and celebratory:** winning, jackpots, promotions, and possibility lead the consumer-facing voice.
- **Direct and action-oriented:** short labels such as “Play now,” “See all games,” “Create Account,” and “Contact Us” dominate.
- **Reassuring and accountable:** safety, regulation, privacy, prize policy, and PlayWise guidance are explicit.
- **Accessible rather than exclusive:** language favors everyday play, help, facts, and clear next actions.

## Anti-references

- Do not describe gambling as risk-free or erase the captured healthy-play and legal context.
- Do not replace Atlantic Canadian ownership and community-return language with generic international gaming language.
- Do not flatten the product families into a single undifferentiated entertainment catalogue.
- Do not treat promotional urgency as the only voice; the current system balances it with trust, support, and accountability.

_provenance: inferred constraints from repeated source messaging; descriptive of current-state boundaries, not a redesign brief._

## Design Principles

1. **Put the next task in reach.** Navigation and CTAs repeatedly foreground play, results, registration, help, and contact.
2. **Keep trust adjacent to action.** Responsible-play, regulated-provider, security, and legal content remain part of the product surface.
3. **Use game identity within a stable corporate frame.** Individual games vary in imagery and color while the ALC header, blue action system, and footer ecosystem recur.
4. **Show regional benefit as a system message.** “Giving Back” and the community-profit statement recur across nearly the full captured inventory.
5. **Support scanning and comparison.** Lists, tiles, summaries, winning numbers, FAQs, and structured form steps are recurring content shapes.
